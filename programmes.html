Programmes

A pathway for every swimmer. Swimmers move up as their skills grow.

Stage 1

Learn to Swim

Water confidence, floating, kicking and first strokes in small groups.

  • Getting comfortable in the water
  • Floating and kicking
  • First strokes
  • Water safety
Stage 2

Stroke Development

Freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly technique.

  • Technique for all four strokes
  • Starts and turns
  • Building distance
Stage 3

Junior Squad

Regular training sessions, fitness and an introduction to galas.

  • Regular squad sessions
  • Fitness and endurance
  • First galas
Stage 4

Competitive Squad

Performance training for swimmers racing at club, provincial and national level.

  • Performance training
  • Race preparation
  • Club, provincial and national galas

Not sure where to start? Get in touch and a coach will advise.
