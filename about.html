About the club

Building confident swimmers, competitive athletes and future champions.

Who we are

A Lusaka club for every stage

Kalene Swim Club is a Lusaka swimming club for young swimmers at every stage, from children who are just getting comfortable in the water to athletes racing at galas.

Our coaches focus on safe, confident swimming first, then on technique, fitness and racing for those who want to compete.

Confidence

Water safety and comfort in the pool come first for every swimmer.

Competition

Squad training and galas for swimmers ready to race.

Champions

Long-term development for swimmers with big goals.
