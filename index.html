About the club

From first strokes to the starting block

Kalene Swim Club is a Lusaka swimming club for young swimmers at every stage, from children who are just getting comfortable in the water to athletes racing at galas.

Water safety and comfort in the pool come first for every swimmer.

Programmes

A pathway for every swimmer

Swimmers move up as their skills grow. Not sure where to start? Get in touch and a coach will advise.

Stage 1

Learn to Swim

Water confidence, floating, kicking and first strokes in small groups.

Stage 2

Stroke Development

Freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly technique.

Stage 3

Junior Squad

Regular training sessions, fitness and an introduction to galas.

Stage 4

Competitive Squad

Performance training for swimmers racing at club, provincial and national level.
